🧬 The Math (Simple)
Your Public RANKD is derived from two hidden numbers:
PUBLIC RANKD =
mu – 2 × phi
μ
mu (Skill Estimate)
Goes up when you win, down when you lose. Beats stronger players = bigger jump.
φ
phi (Uncertainty)
Starts at 350. Shrinks with every match. Lower = the system trusts your number more.
🛡 Why You Can't Farm Rank

Repeated opponents: Beating the same person 10 times teaches the system almost nothing after the first few. Familiarity decay kicks in.

Skill gap: Beating someone 500 points below you barely moves your rating. Losing to them drops it hard.

Integrity weight: Suspicious patterns (same opponent, one-way results, rapid fire) get flagged and reduced.

❓ Quick Answers
Why did I lose points for winning?
You didn't. Your mu went up. But if your phi shrank faster, the public formula (mu – 2×phi) can dip temporarily. It'll correct.
Can I become #1 in 10 matches?
No. Even if you beat the #1 player, you jump big but not past them. You need sustained results against strong, varied opponents.
What if I stop playing?
Your rating stays. You may become "inactive" on leaderboards, but your number doesn't decay. Resume anytime.
